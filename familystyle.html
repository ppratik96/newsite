Key Features 01 / CAPABILITIES
No App Needed for Friends

Send itemized totals and Venmo charge links directly via text. Friends can pay seamlessly with a tap—zero app download required.

Smart OCR Scan

Instantly extracts dish names and prices from your receipt. Multiple quantities are automatically split into individual items for easy assignment.

Flexible Item Splitting

Tap a name once to assign an item, or tap multiple times if someone is covering for others. Shared appetizers split evenly with ease.

Accurate Tax & Tip to the Penny

Tax, tip, and service charges are calculated equitably based on each person's exact subtotal. No manual arithmetic ever required.

Total Control & Reference

Every item name and price is fully editable. You can pop up the original photo receipt on any screen to cross-check anything.

Privacy First & Offline Ready

No accounts, no logins, and zero cloud tracking. Your bill history, friend lists, and settings remain stored strictly locally on your device.
